Entry for the make.fun bounty **The poem about permission**
([the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-permission-20261121-0235)):
a program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to ask
and to be given — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The poem

> I asked for the wheel before I could reach.
> You lifted me up and let me steer,
> your hands hovering,
> until the road felt mine.
>
> Now I drive alone, and the gift I keep
> is not the wheel, not the speed —
> it's knowing the hands are still hovering,
> and I can ask them back.

267 characters, UTF-8, written as a poem about asking, being given the
power to act, and keeping the right to ask for it back.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.txt` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `./out`).
Stdlib only, no network, deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical
output.
